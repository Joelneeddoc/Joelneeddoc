# Graphic design styles

A reference of 25 styles. Each entry gives what defines the style, its palette, type, layout and a
ready-to-use image prompt. Styles 1-5 come from the caption of @ohneis652's Instagram reel
"25 graphic design styles in 40 seconds"; the reel's own prompts were not available, so every
prompt here is written fresh. Styles 6-25 are well-known styles added to complete the set.

## How to use

1. **Pick the style.** If the user names one, use it. If they want ideas, suggest 3 that fit the
   subject and say why in one line each.
2. **Image prompt requested:** take the style's prompt, replace `[SUBJECT]` with the user's subject,
   and adjust the aspect ratio if they need another format. Keep the palette and type words.
3. **Building a page, poster or UI in code:** turn the palette into colour tokens and the type line
   into a font choice (Google Fonts is fine), then follow the layout line. Keep the style's rules
   strict; a style mixed with defaults reads as generic.
4. **Mixing two styles:** take the layout and type from one and the palette from the other, and say
   which is which.

## Styles

### 1. Acid Graphic (from the reel)

Rave-flyer energy: stretched and warped type, chrome and acid-green on black, wireframe rings, glitchy system labels.

- **Palette:** `#0B0B0B`, `#C6FF00`, `#E0E0E0`, `#8A8A8A`
- **Type:** Ultra-condensed grotesk stretched vertically; tiny monospace labels.
- **Layout:** Centered warped wordmark over overlapping thin ellipses; dense micro-text in corners.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Acid Graphic style about [SUBJECT]. Pure black background, acid lime-green and liquid chrome accents. Huge ultra-condensed headline stretched and warped vertically, thin wireframe ellipses and orbit rings crossing the type, tiny monospace system labels and error codes in the corners, subtle digital noise. Rave flyer, Y2K techno, high contrast, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 2. Bauhaus (from the reel)

Primary colours and pure geometry: circle, square, triangle, bars, on a warm off-white ground with lowercase geometric sans.

- **Palette:** `#EFE6D2`, `#D7261E`, `#1D4E9E`, `#F2B705`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Geometric sans (Futura-like), often all lowercase.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric grid of flat primary shapes; text set on a strict horizontal or vertical axis.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Bauhaus style about [SUBJECT]. Warm off-white paper background, flat primary red, blue and yellow with black. Composition built only from circles, squares, triangles and thick bars on an asymmetric grid. Lowercase geometric sans-serif typography on a strict axis. Flat colour, no gradients, no shadows, 1920s Dessau modernism, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 3. Y2K Kawaii (from the reel)

Pastel candy gradients, bubbly rounded type, sparkles, hearts and glossy jelly shapes.

- **Palette:** `#FFD6EC`, `#D9C8FF`, `#C4F1FF`, `#FF5FA8`, `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** Bubbly rounded display face with a thick white outline.
- **Layout:** Centered sticker-like title, scattered sparkles and glossy blobs.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Y2K Kawaii style about [SUBJECT]. Pastel pink, lilac and baby-blue gradient background, glossy jelly bubbles, four-point sparkles, little hearts and stars. Bubbly rounded headline in hot pink with a thick white outline like a sticker. Cute, shiny, early-2000s Japanese pop and flip-phone aesthetic, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 4. Grindhouse (from the reel)

1970s exploitation-film poster: scratched, faded print, blood-red bands, shouty condensed type, rating stamps.

- **Palette:** `#E9B949`, `#B3121B`, `#1A1A1A`, `#F4E9D0`
- **Type:** Heavy condensed all-caps sans, tight leading.
- **Layout:** Stacked headline with a red band, small 'double feature' tagline and a circular stamp.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Grindhouse style about [SUBJECT]. 1970s exploitation-movie poster, faded mustard-yellow paper, blood-red band, black ink. Massive condensed all-caps title, sensational tagline, 'double feature' and rating stamp. Heavy wear: scratches, film grain, folds, ink misregistration and a dark vignette, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 5. Holographic Interface (from the reel)

Sci-fi HUD: iridescent cyan-magenta gradients, thin rings and ticks, scanlines, technical readouts on deep navy.

- **Palette:** `#050A1A`, `#3BF4FB`, `#FF4FD8`, `#B7FF5A`
- **Type:** Wide techno display face; tiny monospace data.
- **Layout:** Concentric HUD rings around a central title with corner readouts.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Holographic Interface style about [SUBJECT]. Deep navy background, iridescent cyan to magenta to lime holographic gradients, thin concentric HUD rings, tick marks, crosshairs and tiny monospace telemetry readouts, faint scanlines and glow. Wide futuristic display type. Sci-fi user interface, translucent layers, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 6. Swiss / International

Grid-driven, objective, flush-left sans type, one strong colour, generous white space.

- **Palette:** `#F4F4F0`, `#E3000F`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Neo-grotesk (Helvetica-like), tight, flush left.
- **Layout:** Strict modular grid; big headline anchored to a column, small text in aligned columns.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Swiss International Typographic Style about [SUBJECT]. Off-white background, black and one strong red. Strict modular grid, huge flush-left neo-grotesk headline with tight tracking, small text set in aligned columns, thin rules. Objective, minimal, 1950s Zurich, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 7. Brutalism

Raw, unpolished and confrontational: default serif and mono, harsh black boxes, concrete grey.

- **Palette:** `#BDBDB8`, `#111111`, `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** Default system serif at huge sizes plus raw monospace.
- **Layout:** Blunt stacked blocks, visible structure, no decoration.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Brutalist style about [SUBJECT]. Concrete grey background, black and white only. Huge default system serif headline, raw monospace labels, harsh black rectangles, visible structure and borders, no decoration, no gradients. Confrontational, unpolished, anti-design, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 8. Neo-Brutalism

Flat bright colours, thick black outlines and hard offset shadows; playful web-app look.

- **Palette:** `#FFF4D6`, `#FFD23F`, `#FF6B9D`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Chunky grotesk, bold, black.
- **Layout:** Outlined cards and stickers with hard drop shadows.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Neo-Brutalism style about [SUBJECT]. Cream background, flat bright yellow and pink, everything outlined in thick black lines with hard 8px offset black shadows, no blur. Chunky bold grotesk type, sticker labels, arrow buttons. Playful modern web-app aesthetic, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 9. Memphis

1980s Memphis Group: squiggles, confetti shapes, dot and zigzag patterns, clashing brights.

- **Palette:** `#FFFFFF`, `#FF5DA2`, `#1FB5AD`, `#FFD000`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Bold geometric sans, playful tilt.
- **Layout:** Scattered shapes and patterns around a tilted title.

```text
Graphic design poster in the 1980s Memphis design style about [SUBJECT]. White background scattered with squiggles, zigzags, confetti triangles, circles and black dot patterns in hot pink, teal, yellow and black. Bold geometric sans headline set at a tilt. Playful, clashing, postmodern, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 10. Art Deco

1920s glamour: gold on deep green or black, sunbursts, strict symmetry, tall stylised capitals.

- **Palette:** `#0F1D1A`, `#C9A24A`, `#F2E6C9`
- **Type:** Tall decorative geometric capitals with inline details.
- **Layout:** Perfectly symmetric, radiating sunburst behind a central title.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Art Deco style about [SUBJECT]. Deep emerald background with metallic gold and cream. Radiating sunburst rays, stepped geometric frames, perfect symmetry. Tall stylised decorative capitals with inline details. 1920s Gatsby glamour, luxurious, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 11. Psychedelic 60s

Swirling vibrating colour, liquid hand-lettering that fills the shape, hot oranges and purples.

- **Palette:** `#FF7A00`, `#D6247F`, `#5B1A8C`, `#FFE14D`
- **Type:** Fat, melting, hand-lettered display type.
- **Layout:** Lettering warps to fill concentric swirls.

```text
Graphic design poster in the 1960s psychedelic concert poster style about [SUBJECT]. Swirling concentric waves of vibrating orange, magenta, purple and yellow. Fat melting hand-lettered headline that warps to fill the space. Op-art vibration, Fillmore poster, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 12. Vaporwave

Pastel pink and cyan, retro computer nostalgia, perspective grids, full-width Latin and Japanese text.

- **Palette:** `#FF71CE`, `#01CDFE`, `#B967FF`, `#FFFB96`
- **Type:** Full-width spaced Latin letters plus Japanese katakana.
- **Layout:** Sunset sun over a perspective grid floor; centred text.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Vaporwave style about [SUBJECT]. Pastel pink to cyan gradient sky, striped sunset sun, magenta perspective grid floor, Greek marble bust and Windows 95 window elements. Full-width spaced letters with Japanese katakana. Dreamy 90s internet nostalgia, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 13. Risograph

Two or three fluorescent spot inks overprinting on paper, grain and slight misregistration.

- **Palette:** `#F6F1E7`, `#FF48B0`, `#0078BF`, `#FFE800`
- **Type:** Bold sans printed in one ink, offset by another.
- **Layout:** Overlapping flat shapes where inks multiply.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Risograph print style about [SUBJECT]. Off-white paper, only fluorescent pink and blue spot inks overprinting and multiplying where they overlap, visible grain, halftone texture and slight misregistration. Bold sans headline, flat overlapping shapes. Indie zine aesthetic, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 14. Constructivism

Soviet avant-garde: red and black diagonals, wedges, photomontage, dynamic angled type.

- **Palette:** `#E8DCC2`, `#C8102E`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Heavy sans caps set on steep diagonals.
- **Layout:** Dynamic diagonal composition with wedges and circles.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Russian Constructivism style about [SUBJECT]. Aged paper background, red and black only. Steep diagonal composition, sharp red wedge piercing a circle, heavy sans-serif capitals set at angles, black-and-white photomontage. 1920s Soviet avant-garde, El Lissitzky and Rodchenko, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 15. Mid-Century Modern

1950s atomic optimism: mustard, teal and orange organic shapes, boomerangs and starbursts.

- **Palette:** `#F1E4C8`, `#D9A441`, `#2A7F7A`, `#D9622B`, `#2B2B2B`
- **Type:** Friendly geometric or brush script; lowercase.
- **Layout:** Loose organic shapes overlapping, lots of breathing room.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Mid-Century Modern style about [SUBJECT]. Warm cream background, mustard, teal, burnt orange and charcoal. Overlapping organic blob shapes, boomerangs and atomic starbursts, slightly textured flat colour. Friendly lowercase type. 1950s atomic-age optimism, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 16. Glassmorphism

Frosted translucent panels floating over blurred colourful orbs; soft light borders.

- **Palette:** `#6A5AE0`, `#FF8BA7`, `#41D3BD`, `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** Clean modern sans, white.
- **Layout:** One frosted card centred over glowing blurred blobs.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Glassmorphism style about [SUBJECT]. Soft glowing blurred orbs of violet, pink and teal in the background, a frosted translucent glass panel in front with a thin bright border and subtle inner light. Clean white modern sans-serif type. Premium UI aesthetic, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 17. Synthwave

Dark 80s neon: striped sunset, chrome and neon glow type, night grid horizon.

- **Palette:** `#1A0033`, `#FF2975`, `#F222FF`, `#FFD319`, `#00F0FF`
- **Type:** Neon outline or chrome script and wide caps.
- **Layout:** Striped sun on the horizon, glowing title above.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Synthwave style about [SUBJECT]. Dark purple night sky, huge striped sunset sun in yellow to hot pink, neon magenta perspective grid, glowing neon outline headline with chrome reflections. 1980s retro-futuristic outrun, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 18. 90s Grunge

Photocopied, torn and taped: distressed typewriter type, xerox noise, marker scrawl.

- **Palette:** `#D9D2C3`, `#1A1A1A`, `#8B1E1E`
- **Type:** Distressed typewriter mixed with scrawled marker.
- **Layout:** Collaged scraps at angles, tape, overlapping text.

```text
Graphic design poster in the 1990s Grunge style about [SUBJECT]. Photocopied paper scraps, torn edges, masking tape, heavy xerox noise and smudges, distressed typewriter type mixed with scrawled marker handwriting, everything slightly crooked. Black and dirty paper with a dull red. Ray Gun magazine, David Carson, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 19. Pop Art

Comic-book Ben-Day dots, thick black outlines, primary brights and shouting speech bubbles.

- **Palette:** `#FFE600`, `#E4002B`, `#0057B8`, `#111111`, `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** Comic lettering, bold caps with outlines.
- **Layout:** Halftone field behind a burst or speech bubble.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Pop Art style about [SUBJECT]. Bright yellow background with red Ben-Day halftone dots, thick black outlines, primary red and blue, a white comic speech bubble with a shouted bold headline. Roy Lichtenstein, Andy Warhol, 1960s comic print, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 20. Art Nouveau

Flowing organic lines, arched frames, botanical ornament, muted olive and gold.

- **Palette:** `#EFE3C8`, `#5A6B3A`, `#B08D3C`, `#3B2A1E`
- **Type:** Decorative flowing display type.
- **Layout:** Arched frame with whiplash curves; title on a banner.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Art Nouveau style about [SUBJECT]. Cream background with muted olive, gold and deep brown. Arched decorative frame, flowing whiplash curves, stylised flowers and vines, ornamental flowing lettering. Alphonse Mucha, 1900s Paris, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 21. Japanese Minimalism

Vast negative space, one red mark, vertical serif text, calm paper tones.

- **Palette:** `#F3EFE6`, `#C8102E`, `#1E1E1E`
- **Type:** Mincho serif, vertical writing.
- **Layout:** Off-centre single element, thin line, lots of empty space.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Japanese Minimalism style about [SUBJECT]. Warm paper background, mostly empty space, one small red circle placed off-centre, a single thin black line, vertical Japanese Mincho serif text and tiny Latin caption. Calm, quiet, Ma and wabi-sabi, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 22. Cyberpunk

Neon yellow and magenta on near-black, angular cut corners, glitch RGB split, kanji signage.

- **Palette:** `#0D0221`, `#FCEE0A`, `#FF2A6D`, `#05D9E8`
- **Type:** Wide techno caps with RGB-split glitch.
- **Layout:** Angular clipped panels, warning stripes, overlaid signage.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Cyberpunk style about [SUBJECT]. Near-black background, neon yellow, hot magenta and cyan. Angular clipped panels with cut corners, hazard stripes, glitched RGB-split wide techno headline, glowing kanji signage, rain and city neon. Dystopian future, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 23. Dada Collage

Ransom-note letters from mixed fonts, cut-out newsprint, random angles, red and black.

- **Palette:** `#E9E4D8`, `#111111`, `#C8102E`, `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** Every letter a different cut-out font.
- **Layout:** Chaotic collage of fragments at random rotations.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Dada collage style about [SUBJECT]. Cut-out newspaper fragments, ransom-note headline where every letter is a different font and size, random rotations, black and red ink on yellowed newsprint, scissor-cut edges and glue marks. 1910s Dada absurdist photomontage, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 24. Blueprint

Technical drawing: white line work on cyanotype blue, grid, dimensions and a title block.

- **Palette:** `#1B4F8A`, `#FFFFFF`, `#9CC3EE`
- **Type:** Technical monospace and stencil labels.
- **Layout:** Centred line drawing with dimension lines and a corner title block.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Blueprint technical drawing style about [SUBJECT]. Cyanotype blue background with a faint white grid, precise white line drawing of the subject with dimension lines, measurements in millimetres, construction circles and a title block in the corner. Engineering schematic, monospace labels, 4:5 portrait.
```

### 25. Op Art

Black-and-white optical illusions: concentric rings, warped stripes, moiré vibration.

- **Palette:** `#FFFFFF`, `#111111`
- **Type:** Minimal small sans; the pattern is the hero.
- **Layout:** Full-bleed geometric pattern with an optical bulge or shift.

```text
Graphic design poster in the Op Art style about [SUBJECT]. Strict black and white only. Full-bleed optical illusion of concentric rings and warped stripes that appear to bulge and vibrate, moiré effects, precise geometry, small minimal sans-serif caption. Bridget Riley, Victor Vasarely, 4:5 portrait.
```
